What this model cannot tell us yet.

Households reported their own circumstances.Answers may be incomplete, inaccurate, or influenced by how questions are asked.
The data comes from the pandemic.Performance on today’s bank customers has not been established.
Identifying a reported delay is not predicting a new missed payment.Later survey reports may include an existing delay.
A flag is a reason to assess, not a verdict.False alarms and missed cases remain. The ensemble also reused an earlier holdout.

Before deployment, a pilot should prove two things.

1Validate the warning
  • Precision and recall at equal outreach capacity
  • Lift versus the current approach
  • Warning time and manageable alert volume
2Test the support
  • Fewer missed payments versus a control group
  • Positive net benefit after costs
Recommendation

Validate a bank-data pilot before operational deployment.

References and notes

Data. World Bank COVID-19 High-Frequency Phone Survey, Philippines, 2020–2021. Unweighted analysis. Households self-reported payment delays; the question has no fixed recall window.

Study. https://aid504-project.vercel.app/philippines/covid-payment-delays. Records from one household stayed in one group. Identification holdout: 2,262 interviews from 1,512 households. Forecasting holdout: 361 households. Macro ROC-AUC averages per-round scores.

Featured model. 15-input stacked ensemble: logistic regression, LightGBM and CatBoost scores combined by a final logistic regression. Holdout macro ROC-AUC 0.788 (95% CI 0.764–0.811). The holdout had already been used for earlier models, so this is a consistency check, not an untouched test. Balanced cutoff 0.63 (Youden's J) set on development data before holdout scoring. Permutation importance from the same model; the coping count excludes the delayed-payment item and overlaps with individual coping inputs.

Prototype. https://aid504-project.vercel.app/philippines/early-warning. Lender workspace screenshots captured 18 Nov 2026 from sample households. The prototype scores applications with the 15-input stack refitted on Round 1 and Round 2 interviews (development cross-validation macro ROC-AUC 0.797; no holdout check). The phone demo on slide 9 uses the prototype's questions but does not save or score answers.

Images. Photographs are generated illustrations, not survey participants or bank customers. The bank dashboard and phone message use fictional sample data.
